5 Scenario: The BMS Trust Trap

During service, a battery pack over-temperature warning flashed briefly, then cleared on its own — the BMS limited current and activated cooling. Is "the warning is gone" the same as "the problem is solved"? Investigate before you decide.

📋 Based on published BYD Blade Battery / BMS technical facts (LFP Cell-to-Pack, thermal-runaway threshold, active/passive cell balancing)
Diagnostic time spent: 0 min

Step 1 — Choose your next action:

A. Pull the warning history (DTC log) from the diagnostic tool (4 min)
B. Inspect the cooling system condition (5 min)
C. Check the cell-balancing history (3 min)
D. Warning is gone — call it normal and hand the vehicle back

Step 2 — What is the correct judgment?

A. LFP's thermal-runaway threshold (~270°C) is higher than NMC's (~210°C), so a cleared warning means it's fine to drive normally
B. A cleared warning only means the BMS is suppressing the symptom — don't return the vehicle to normal service until the root cause is found
C. This is a Cell-to-Pack design — since cells are integrated into the pack without modules, an individual cell fault is safe
Choose an action to begin.

📘 Glossary for some abbreviations

Key terms and acronyms used throughout this training module

EV: Electric Vehicle
HV: High Voltage
VIN: Vehicle Identification Number
HEV: Hybrid Electric Vehicle
Voltage Drop: The decrease in electrical energy that happens as electricity flows through a circuit.
CPR: Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation
BMS: Battery Management System
LFP: Lithium Ferro Phosphate
DTC: Diagnostic Trouble Code
NMC: Nickel Manganese Cobalt
DC-DC Converter: Converts the level of DC voltage
PDU: Power Distribution Unit
LOTO: Lock Out Tag Out
DOT: Department of Transportation
HVAC: Heating Ventilation and Air Conditioning
IGBT: Insulated-Gate Bipolar Transistor
CAN: Controller Area Network
